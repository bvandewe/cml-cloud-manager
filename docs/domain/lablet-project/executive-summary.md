# Project Overview

## Executive Summary

The CML Lablets Project represents a strategic transformation of Cisco certification laboratory environments from legacy IOL (IOS on Linux) systems to modern CML (Cisco Modeling Labs) infrastructure. This initiative delivers enhanced capabilities, improved scalability, and greater network topology flexibility for modern certification delivery.

## Vision & Mission

### Vision

To become the premier platform for Cisco certification hands-on assessment experiences, providing scalable, reliable, and cost-effective laboratory environments that enhance learner outcomes and operational efficiency.

### Mission

Deliver a comprehensive CML-based platform that:

- Reduces lab initialization time from minutes to seconds
- Supports concurrent multi-user scenarios at scale
- Provides consistent, reproducible assessment environments
- Enables advanced networking scenarios impossible with legacy IOL
- Optimizes infrastructure costs through intelligent resource management

## Project Scope & Boundaries

### In Scope

- 6 Lablet Definitions for core APS certification tracks (DEVASC, AUTOCOR, SCOR, CLCOR, DCCOR)
- CML platform integration and optimization
- Resource management and pooling systems
- Modern architecture migration (perl-LDS to pyLDS + Mozart)
- Advanced cost optimization and multi-tenancy

### Out of Scope (Current Phases)

- Non-APS certification tracks
- Major changes to existing PVUE/ALII protocols
- Complete replacement of legacy systems during Phase 1
- Advanced AI/ML features beyond cost optimization

## Risk Management Overview

### Strategic Risks
